# Stops by itself when you stop talking

- Dictation and translation stop after 5 seconds of silence, with a short countdown you can talk over. Adjust it from 1 to 10 seconds in Settings, or turn it off.
- Meetings have their own silence setting: they stop after 60 seconds of quiet by default, adjustable up to 5 minutes.
- Local Whisper is faster: it runs on a newer engine and starts loading the model as soon as you start recording.
