# Meetings that record themselves

- Five minutes before a meeting a small window in the middle of the screen shows what's next; the full-screen screen still comes at the start.
- Join now records by default: audio only during the call, transcribed on your Mac after it ends. A live transcript can be turned on mid-meeting.
- When the call ends, Diduny offers to stop recording; drop out and rejoin, and the recording just continues.
